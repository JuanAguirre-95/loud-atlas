Loud Atlas is an interactive map of rock and metal history, from the Delta blues to today: how bands and artists, their releases, genres and subgenres, influences and shared members connect.

View the source on GitHubJuanAguirre-95/loud-atlas

Spotted a wrong date or a missing band? Open an issue or send a pull request. The data is plain JSON in data/.

Keyboard

  • Tab to the map, then J / K for the next or previous act, Enter for its details.
  • ← → ↑ ↓ pan, + - zoom, 0 fit, Esc clears.
  • / search, L list view, T switch theme.
  • Focus the time strip and use the arrow keys, + and -.

Using the map

  • Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, Shift+scroll to zoom time only.
  • Click a band for its summary, releases, influences and lineup. Its tags and filter buttons narrow the map.
  • The time brush at the bottom pans and zooms time. The ruler picks a decade (top row) or a year (bottom row).
  • Open a genre's arrow in Filters to pick subgenres. Alt+click shows only one.
  • Explore has four tools: browse a year, see what was released today in history, connect any two acts through influences and shared members, or compare up to three.
  • The sun and moon button switches between the dark and light themes. L shows everything as a text list.

How accurate is it?

Years active and release years were checked against independent sources (Wikipedia, Wikidata and MusicBrainz), and the key releases are chosen by rule: the best community-rated albums, or the most listened when that data is built in. The genre and subgenre of an act, and the influence lines, are judgement calls rather than facts. Album lists come from MusicBrainz and may include regional editions for 1960s acts. See something wrong? Tell us.
